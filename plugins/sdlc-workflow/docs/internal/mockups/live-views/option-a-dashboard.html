Yolo run view

One focus: the slice and stage that run now, with a heartbeat that says the run is alive.

One queue: "Needs you" holds only what waits for you: an intent-bearing decision or a changed protected file. Most of the time it is empty.

Everything else (agents, commits, steering proof) is one quiet line. Turn details on to see all of it.

Hotkeys 1 and 2 stop the run at the next boundary.

Replaces the hand-built Monitor watch: no re-arm, no polling in chat.

  1. 1
    Heartbeat. Pulses at each journal line. It slows as the gap grows, turns amber at half the stale limit and red at the limit. Q1
  2. 2
    Stage rail. One track per slice; the runner slides when a stage ends. The bar under it compares time used with the usual time. Q2
  3. 3
    Agent lanes. Stage agents are bright. Scouts and refuters are dim lines. Q1 Q2
  4. 4
    Commit ticker. New commits slide in. Q2
  5. 5
    Decision card. Intent-bearing decisions are violet and stay until you open the record. Q3 Q4
  6. 6
    Shields. A shield breaks when a protected file's hash changes. A toast tells you. Q4
  7. 7
    Steering proof. One square per agent that read steer.md after your last edit. Q2
  8. 8
    Turn line. The Spinner shows slice, stage and elapsed time. Q2
  9. 9
    Band with stop buttons. The mod writes .control.json; agents stop at the next boundary. Try hotkey 1 or 2. Q4
  10. 10
    Usage in the status line. From the usage guard mod. Q5
Terminal: the rails and heartbeat draw in a Client region on its frame clock. Desktop: the same tree; the rails as Svg. The data comes from .driver-journal.jsonl and stage records through $.fs on a $.clock timer: no tokens.

Campaign view

One board: waves are columns. Done slugs fold to one line. Gate lights show only on the active wave.

Needs you holds prepare requests. Usage is two bars with the 75 and 90 lines. Outputs gives the PR per wave and the build to try.

At 90% the campaign pauses at the boundary and resumes by itself. Hotkey r resumes now.

The whole campaign on one board: waves, gates, cost, the stack and the outputs.

  1. 1
    Wave board. Columns are waves; cells are slugs with a small stage rail. Lines are depends-on; a blue flowing line feeds a running slug. Under each wave, six gate lights: Merge, Verify, Drift, Refuter, Fidelity, Ship. Amber cells wait for your prepare. Q2 Q4
  2. 2
    Usage gauges. 5-hour and weekly arcs with the 75 and 90 lines. The forecast bar compares what the remaining waves need with what is left. At 90 the campaign pauses at the boundary and counts down to the reset. Q5
  3. 3
    Heavy-suite lock. Who holds the Playwright lock, and how long the others wait. Q1
  4. 4
    PR stack and outputs. One PR per wave with its CI state, the build label wave-<n>+<sha>, and the version at ship. The try-it card gives the output path. Q2 Q4
  5. 5
    Band. One line for the wave and buttons for prepare and stop. Q4

Brainstorm view

One map. Ideas fade in as you talk. During the walk each leaf takes your call: green keep, red cut, grey later.

At done the map turns into the packet graph, with size bars marked at 25 and 40 decisions.

Folders and the change log are behind details.

The talk becomes a map you can see grow, then a packet graph you can check before the campaign runs.

  1. 1
    Idea map → packet graph. Threads are branches; each captured item fades in on its branch. During the scoping walk each leaf takes your call: keep, cut or later. At done the map turns into the work packets: one node per slug, edges for depends-on, columns for the waves. Each node has a size bar with marks at 25 and 40 carried decisions. Over 40 is red and must split. Q2 Q3 Q4
  2. 2
    Mode and walk progress. Explore, scope, done. Only you end the brainstorm. Q2
  3. 3
    Folders. research/, references/ and work/ fill as the session writes them. Q2
  4. 4
    Band. The last captured item, so you see what the session heard. Q3
At the end the brainstorm is reopened: a hotfix packet arrives with amends, the revision goes 3 → 4, and the new node pulses. The campaign picks it up from work/changes.md.

Mobile surface · Svg summary card (in the Claude app)
Which events reach the phone
Only events that change what you do next: a decision, a protected-file change, a stale run, a stop or pause, a wave ready with its try-it note, a prepare waiting, and the run end. Stage ends, commits and re-arms never notify.

Phone and push

You leave the desk. The run keeps you in the loop without noise.

  1. 1
    Push notifications carry one decision each, with the next action. Q4
  2. 2
    Summary card. The mobile surface draws Svg, Box, Text and Button. The ring shows the campaign progress; the bars show both usage windows. Q2 Q5
The mobile table has no Input or Select, so answers to a decision go through Buttons or the chat.
How real is this? Each element maps to a drawing element in the Claude Code 2.1.286 mod API: Pane (docked in fullscreen from 110 columns), the AbovePrompt band with hotkey Buttons, the Spinner turn line, $.ui.status, $.ui.toast, terminal Raster cells repainted by $.ui.blit (about 60 frames a second), Client regions with their own frame clock (terminal and desktop), and Svg with SMIL animation on desktop, VS Code and mobile. The page uses web fonts and CSS; a terminal draws the same layout in cells, with 24-bit colour, block and braille characters. Nothing here is built.
